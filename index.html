About the company

From field operations to digital intelligence.

Agri-Technology Africa is an agricultural technology initiative focused on making modern drone services practical and accessible to farms, farmer groups, agribusinesses, researchers and development projects.

  • Field-focused drone operations
  • Digital farm mapping and measurement
  • Aerial crop monitoring and scouting support
  • AI-assisted agricultural data workflows
  • Training and responsible technology adoption
