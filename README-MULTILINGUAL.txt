Agri-Technology Africa — Multilingual Website

Default language: English.
Available languages: English, Twi, Français.

The language selector appears at the top of every page. The selected language is remembered in the browser. When the visitor returns to the home page after choosing Twi or Français, the preferred language opens automatically.

Folders:
- English pages are at the package root (default).
- Twi pages are in /Twi/.
- French pages are in /French/.
