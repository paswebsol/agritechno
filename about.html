About Agri-Technology Africa

A Ghana-focused agricultural technology initiative using drones, digital data and practical field services to support smarter farming.

Our story

Making aerial intelligence useful on the ground.

Agri-Technology Africa is designed around a simple principle: technology should solve real farm problems. We are developing a professional service platform around agricultural drone operations, mapping, crop observation, precision application, AI-assisted analysis and training.

Our ambition is to grow from a Ghana-focused operation into a scalable African agricultural technology service, supporting commercial farms, cooperatives, smallholder groups, researchers and development programs.

Mission

Deliver reliable, responsible and practical drone-enabled agricultural services that improve field visibility and decision-making.

Vision

Become a trusted African agricultural technology platform connecting farms with aerial intelligence and digital tools.

Values

Safety, integrity, innovation, farmer value, responsible technology and continuous learning.
