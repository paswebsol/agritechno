Mmuaeɛ tiawa a ɛfa Agri-Technology Africa afuo drone nnwuma ho.

Agri-Technology Africa yɛ dɛn?

Yɛyɛ afuo drone nnwuma a ɛka nnuru-pete nhyehyɛeɛ, afifideɛ hwɛ, mapa, AI mmoa nhwehwɛmu, pɛpɛɛpɛ nnɔbaeɛ mmoa ne nteteeɛ ho.

Mo tumi ne nketewa afuofoɔ yɛ adwuma?

Yɛn nhyehyɛeɛ no boa ankorankoro afuofoɔ, afuofoɔ akuw ne afuo nnwuma akɛseɛ, sɛ beaeɛ ne adwuma no ahwehwɛdeɛ ma kwan a.

Drone tumi hu mmoawa ne nyarewa?

Drone tumi yi mfoni a ɛkyerɛ nsusuwsoɔ a ɛbata afifideɛ haw ho. Ɛsɛ sɛ yɛfa nea yɛhunu no sɛ ahwɛeɛ a edi kan anaa mmeaeɛ a ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ no yiye, na wɔsi so dua denam afuo mu nhwehwɛmu anaa yareɛ ho nhwehwɛmu a ɔfata so.

Nsɛm bɛn na ɛsɛ sɛ mede ma?

Afuo beaeɛ, nnɔbaeɛ, afuo kɛseɛ a ɛbɛn, adwuma a wopɛ, da a wopɛ ne afuo mu akwansideɛ biara a ɛho hia.

Wɔhwɛ drone dwumadie so wɔ Ghana?

Yiw. Ghana Civil Aviation Authority wɔ ahwehwɛdeɛ a ɛfa RPAS drone dwumadie ho. Aguadiɛ mu dwumadiefoɔ ɛsɛ sɛ wɔhwɛ drone din-kyerɛw, tumi krataa, adansedie, dwumadie ne mmeaeɛ a wɔmma drone nkɔ ho ahwehwɛdeɛ a ɛwɔ hɔ saa bere no ansa na wɔayɛ adwuma.
